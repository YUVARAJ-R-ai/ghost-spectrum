LINK DOCTOR — Circuit & Wiring

Three ESP32 boards. Pin labels match the firmware. Flash one board at a time. Full build/flash/demo steps are in README.md; this page is wiring.

3V3 5V (VIN) GND I²C radio data control sensor

Signal flow

Node A — IoT device (ESP32)

GPIO 34 is deliberate — it's an ADC1 pin. ADC2 pins stop reading the instant Wi-Fi turns on, so the analog sensor must stay on ADC1 (GPIO 32–39, input-only).
ComponentPin → ESP32 (Node A)
FS1000A 433 TXVCC→VIN (5V) · GND→GND · DATA→GPIO17 · ANT→17.3 cm wire
Buzzer module (Active)VCC→3V3 · GND→GND · I/O→GPIO25
IR proximity moduleVCC→3V3 · GND→GND · AO (analog out)→GPIO34
Momentary PushbuttonPin 1→GPIO27 · Pin 2→GND (Internal pullup active)

Node B — radio gateway (ESP8266)

Power the 433 receiver from 3V3, not 5 V. At 5 V its DATA line outputs ~5 V and would stress/damage the ESP8266's 3.3 V input. 3V3 provides safe 3.3 V logic levels and is more than sufficient for desk range.
ComponentPin → ESP8266 (Node B / NodeMCU)
SSD1306 I²C OLED (0x3C)VCC→3V3 · GND→GND · SCL→D1 (GPIO5) · SDA→D2 (GPIO4)
XY-MK-5V 433 RXVCC→3V3 (Safe logic) · GND→GND · DATA→D5 (GPIO14) · ANT→17.3 cm wire
RadioHead ReservedD6 (GPIO12) & D7 (GPIO13) reserved by RH_ASK driver (leave unconnected)
Node B receives 16-byte frames from Node A over 433 MHz, validates CRC16 and sequence integrity, renders live metrics on the OLED, and forwards JSON telemetry over Wi-Fi UDP and USB Serial to the laptop.

Node C — air watcher (ESP32, optional)

Plain ESP32 on USB. Nothing wired to it. Counts Wi-Fi traffic and deauth frames on the hotspot's channel.

Leave the NRF24 OFF. It caused the flashing failure (invalid header: 0xffffffff) by loading the 3.3 V rail. Skip this whole node if short on time — the demo works with A and B alone.

Build order

  1. Breadboard Node A (ESP32): connect FS1000A transmitter (VCC to VIN/5V, DATA to GPIO 17), active buzzer (I/O to GPIO 25), IR sensor analog out (AO to GPIO 34), and momentary pushbutton between GPIO 27 and GND. Solder a 17.3 cm wire to the FS1000A ANT pad.
  2. Breadboard Node B (ESP8266): connect SSD1306 OLED (SDA to D2/GPIO4, SCL to D1/GPIO5, VCC to 3V3, GND to GND), XY-MK-5V receiver (VCC to 3V3 for safe logic level, DATA to D5/GPIO14, GND to GND). Solder a 17.3 cm wire to the receiver ANT pad.
  3. Keep the two 433 antennas ~20 cm apart and vertical.
  4. Flash each board (see README.md). Find ports with ls /dev/ttyUSB*.
  5. Power Node A from a power bank or USB, and Node B from USB.
